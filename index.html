StreamForge

A private desktop tool that turns gameplay recordings into YouTube Shorts and mission videos for the channel GTA 6 Welt.

What it does

  • Finds the best moments in a gameplay recording: funny moments, fails, hidden places, settings and tips.
  • Edits them into vertical Shorts with captions, and edits missions into longer videos.
  • Checks every video for quality, then uploads the approved ones to the owner's YouTube channel on a schedule.

StreamForge runs on the channel owner's own Windows PC. It is not a public service and has no user accounts.

Why it asks for Google sign-in

StreamForge uses YouTube API Services for two things only:

  • Upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel, with their title, description, tags and thumbnail.
  • Read the channel's name and handle, to confirm that the sign-in belongs to the right channel before anything is uploaded.

It does not read, change or delete existing videos, comments, playlists or account settings. The full details are in the privacy policy.
