Privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026. This policy explains what StreamForge does with data, including data it receives from Google.

Who this applies to

StreamForge is a desktop tool that the owner of the YouTube channel GTA 6 Welt runs on their own Windows PC to make and upload videos. It is not offered to the public. The only person who signs in with Google is the channel owner.

YouTube API Services

StreamForge uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's own handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What StreamForge accesses in the Google account

PermissionWhat it is used for
Manage your YouTube videos
youtube.upload		Uploading new videos to the channel with their title, description, tags, privacy setting, scheduled time and thumbnail.
View your YouTube account
youtube.readonly		Reading the channel's ID, name and handle once after sign-in and when the connection is tested, to confirm the sign-in belongs to the intended channel.

StreamForge does not read, edit or delete existing videos, comments, playlists, subscriptions, analytics or account settings, and it does not access Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google product.

What is stored, and where

  • The sign-in token that Google issues is stored in the Windows Credential Manager on the owner's PC. It is used only to make the API calls listed above.
  • The channel's ID, name and handle and the IDs of videos that were uploaded are stored in a local database file on the same PC.
  • Nothing is sent to a server run by the developer. StreamForge has no server, no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Sharing

Data received from Google is not sold, not shared with anyone and not used for advertising. It is sent only to Google's own YouTube API, to carry out the uploads the owner asked for.

StreamForge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data received from Google is not used to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Other services the tool can use

These are optional and do not receive any data from the Google account:

  • Anthropic (Claude) can receive text from the recordings being edited, such as what was said in a clip, to rank clips and write titles.
  • ElevenLabs can receive short narration text to turn into speech in the owner's own voice.
  • Twitch, Kick and YouTube public APIs can be asked whether a creator is live, and public YouTube comments on a video can be read to find timestamps viewers mention. These calls use public data and do not use the Google sign-in.

Keeping and deleting data

  • The sign-in token is kept until the owner presses Disconnect in StreamForge, which deletes it from the PC, or until Google expires it.
  • Access can also be removed at any time in the Google Account under Third-party apps and services.
  • The local database and all generated files can be deleted by deleting StreamForge's data folder.

Security

The sign-in token never leaves the owner's PC except in encrypted (HTTPS) requests to Google. StreamForge's control panel only answers on the local machine and refuses requests from other websites.

Children

StreamForge is a tool for one adult channel owner. It is not directed at children and does not collect data from viewers of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the channel owner through the GTA 6 Welt YouTube channel. The support email address is also shown on the Google sign-in screen for StreamForge.
